Questions

Asked and answered.

What is the Greenroom Network?

A wellness entertainment network. Streaming shows, live shopping, recovery content and community in one room. Sports, culture, comedy and plant wellness for grown folks, side by side.

When does it launch?

Winter 2026. Founding members get in first, with previews and live tapings before the public doors open.

What does the $1.99 reservation actually get me?

Your first month free when the doors open. A founding rate for life below every public price, announced before launch and guaranteed below the $6.99 public rate. Your name on the Founding Wall and first access to drops and live tapings. The $1.99 is refundable any time before launch, and if you keep your seat it is credited to your first paid month after the free one. If the doors never open, every reservation is refunded automatically.

What is the difference between the free list and a founding seat?

The free list gets the launch letter, previews and first looks. A founding seat adds the first month free, the rate lock for life, the Founding Wall and first access to drops and tapings. Finishing the Vibe Check separately earns an Insider credit of $10 for the content store at launch.

Do you sell cannabis on this site?

No. This site sells nothing but a seat. Live drops feature legal goods only. Plant wellness content sits in a 21 and over lane.

How does live shopping work?

You watch the show. A product appears on screen. You tap it and it is yours. Products come from the people who make the shows, with member deals while the broadcast runs.

How does the $150 gift card drawing work?

Finish the Vibe Check before launch day and you are entered. One winner drawn at launch and notified by email. NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. Open to US residents 21 and over. Approximate retail value $150. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries. Read the Official Rules.

Why does watching live take me to a different site right now?

Greenroom programming streams on our live network home while thegreenroomnetwork.com finishes construction. Everything moves here when the doors open this winter. Your founding seat and credits carry over on day one.
